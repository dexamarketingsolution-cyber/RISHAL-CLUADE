About

Design that sells and stays on brand

I'm Rilshan, a graphic designer with more than a decade across branding, advertising, print production and digital marketing.

My career started on the print floor, preparing artwork for offset presses in the UAE and Qatar. That's where I learned bleeds, dielines and colour that holds up on paper. Since then I've led social media design for a Doha law firm, built marketing material for trading companies and run marketing for a distributor in Sri Lanka. I design with the sales goal in view, because I've worked on that side too.

Today I combine Adobe Creative Suite with AI-assisted tools to concept faster, then finish every piece by hand to brand standard.
